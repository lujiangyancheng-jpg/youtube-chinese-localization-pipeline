# Copyright policy

Video Localizer is designed for owned, licensed, public-domain, and otherwise lawfully usable media. It does not grant rights in source material and does not bypass DRM, paywalls, authentication, private-video controls, or regional access controls.

Every desktop job asks the user to declare a rights basis. The project folder then includes `RIGHTS_RECORD.json`, `RIGHTS_RECORD.md`, and `ATTRIBUTION.txt`. These files are an audit aid based on user-supplied information; they are not proof of ownership or legal advice.

Before publishing, verify that the applicable authorization covers downloading, translation or adaptation, redistribution, the intended territory and platform, and commercial use where relevant. Preserve required attribution, license links, change notices, and share-alike terms.

Repository issues should not contain infringing media, private URLs, credentials, or personal data. A copyright concern about repository content can be reported through GitHub's copyright process; concerns about a user's independently processed output must be directed to that user or the platform hosting the output.
