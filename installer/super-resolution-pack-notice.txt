AI Super Resolution is optional and runs entirely on this computer.

It installs the portable waifu2x-ncnn-vulkan runtime and two curated 2x restoration model sets:
- photo/general video
- animation/line-art video

The application processes frames in bounded batches and probes available Vulkan devices in turn.
NVIDIA, AMD, Intel, and a slower CPU fallback are supported when the runtime can initialize them.

AI restoration estimates plausible detail; it cannot recover information that was never captured.
Keep the original source and review the result before publishing.
